Prices are English-language Near Mint (or Mint) single-card listings on CardTrader, in EUR, without shipping, captured once a day. Reference price = middle of the 5 cheapest copies (at most 2 per seller); cheapest = the single cheapest copy. Less expensive cards are charted once a week. Prices are as of the snapshot date, not live. Not affiliated with CardTrader.
